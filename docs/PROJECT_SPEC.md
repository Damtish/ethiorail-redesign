# EthioRail Website Redesign — Project Specification

## 1. Project Overview

Ethio Rail Logistics requires a complete redesign of its existing public website.

This is not a visual refresh of the current website.

The new website should be rebuilt from the ground up as a modern, premium, professional digital presence for an Ethiopian logistics company operating across rail, road, ocean freight, freight forwarding, customs, warehousing, dry-port and multimodal logistics services.

The website should present Ethio Rail Logistics as:

- modern
- trustworthy
- operationally capable
- internationally connected
- technologically forward-looking
- authentically Ethiopian
- capable of serving corporate and institutional logistics customers

The design should be comparable in quality and professionalism to leading international logistics websites while maintaining its own EthioRail identity.

---

## 2. Core Project Goal

Create a high-quality corporate logistics website that:

1. Clearly explains what Ethio Rail Logistics does.
2. Presents its services professionally.
3. Builds confidence with prospective customers and partners.
4. Makes requesting logistics services easy.
5. Communicates the Ethiopia–Djibouti and international logistics connection.
6. Uses strong authentic and cinematic logistics media.
7. Works extremely well on desktop, tablet and mobile.
8. Supports English and Amharic architecture.
9. Is SEO-friendly and performant.
10. Is designed so future customer and employee portals can be added without rebuilding the public website.

---

## 3. Product Vision

The website should feel like the digital platform of a serious international logistics company rather than a generic corporate template.

The experience should communicate:

“Moving Ethiopia. Connecting the World.”

This phrase is currently a working creative direction and must not be treated as final approved corporate copy unless later approved.

The overall product should combine:

- industrial scale
- international trade
- Ethiopian identity
- rail logistics
- multimodal transportation
- technology
- simplicity
- credibility

---

## 4. Target Audiences

### Primary audiences

#### Importers and exporters
Businesses moving goods into or out of Ethiopia.

#### Corporate logistics customers
Companies requiring regular freight, transportation, warehousing or customs services.

#### Manufacturers
Companies requiring inbound materials and outbound product logistics.

#### International logistics partners
Freight forwarders, shipping companies, NVOCC partners, agents and other logistics organizations.

#### Institutional customers
Government, NGOs, international organizations and large commercial organizations requiring logistics services.

### Secondary audiences

- potential employees
- business partners
- investors/stakeholders
- government and regulatory stakeholders
- media
- general visitors researching the company

---

## 5. Primary User Goals

A visitor should be able to quickly:

- understand what Ethio Rail Logistics does
- understand the available services
- request a quote
- contact the company
- understand the company’s logistics network
- learn about the Ethiopia–Djibouti corridor
- find offices/contact information
- review routes or schedules when available
- learn about the company
- access news/resources
- find careers
- eventually access the customer portal

The site should prioritize practical customer actions rather than forcing users to read large amounts of corporate text.

## 12. Visual Experience

The website should feel:

- premium
- industrial
- clean
- confident
- contemporary
- international
- Ethiopian
- highly visual

Use:

- large logistics photography
- cinematic video backgrounds where appropriate
- authentic Ethiopian railway imagery
- container/rail/port imagery
- strong typography
- generous whitespace
- carefully controlled motion
- maps and route visualizations
- strong information hierarchy

Avoid:

- generic WordPress logistics-template appearance
- excessive rounded cards
- excessive gradients
- excessive glassmorphism
- random stock icons
- fake statistics
- fake customer data
- fake shipment tracking
- unnecessary animations
- excessive text
- generic AI-looking interfaces
- visual clutter

## 13. Media Strategy

The project already contains an organized media library under:

public/media/

Media includes:

- hero/background freight video
- port footage
- rail footage
- warehouse footage
- genuine Ethiopian railway photography
- generic rail stock photography
- Addis Ababa imagery
- port imagery

## 14. Authenticity Requirements

Do not invent:

- company statistics
- customer counts
- shipment volumes
- network size
- years of experience
- destinations
- certifications
- clients
- partners
- offices
- routes
- schedules
- licenses
- awards
- leadership information

Any corporate fact that is not verified should remain a placeholder until confirmed.

The website must never sacrifice factual accuracy for visual impact.

---

## 15. Content Principles

Content should be:

- concise
- professional
- confident
- specific
- customer-focused
- easy to scan
- credible

Avoid corporate filler such as:

“We provide the best quality solutions for all your logistics needs.”

Prefer useful explanations that tell visitors:

- what the service is
- how it works
- where it operates
- why it matters
- what action the customer should take

---

## 16. Responsive Requirement

The website must be designed intentionally for:

- large desktop
- standard desktop
- laptop
- tablet
- mobile

Mobile must not be treated as a compressed desktop layout.

Navigation, typography, video, images, maps, forms and interactive sections should have deliberate mobile behavior.

---

## 17. Accessibility

The site should follow modern accessibility practices, including:

- semantic HTML
- keyboard navigation
- appropriate contrast
- visible focus states
- form labels
- alt text
- reduced-motion consideration
- accessible navigation
- accessible interactive controls

---

## 18. Performance

Performance is a major requirement.

The final implementation should consider:

- optimized images
- optimized video
- responsive image delivery
- lazy loading
- efficient fonts
- minimized client-side JavaScript
- proper caching
- Core Web Vitals

Important:

The original media assets should NOT be optimized during the current planning phase.

Optimization will happen later after final asset usage is known.

---

## 19. SEO

The website should support:

- semantic page structure
- descriptive URLs
- page metadata
- Open Graph metadata
- sitemap
- robots configuration
- structured data when appropriate
- canonical URLs
- localization metadata
- readable headings
- image alt text

No keyword stuffing.

---

## 20. Security

The public site should follow standard web security practices.

Particular attention will later be required for:

- forms
- file uploads if introduced
- API endpoints
- customer authentication
- employee authentication
- role-based authorization
- customer documents
- customer data

Future portal functionality must be treated as a secure application, not simply hidden website pages.
